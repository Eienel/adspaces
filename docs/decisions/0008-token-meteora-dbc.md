# ADR-0008: Platform token on Meteora DBC with virality-based burns

- **Status:** Proposed (Phase 4, blocked on legal review)
- **Date:** 2026-10-28
- **Deciders:** Owner

## Context
The owner wants a token launched on Meteora's Dynamic Bonding Curve, with burns tied to ad virality.

## Decision (proposed)
Launch via Meteora DBC; fund burns from a share of platform fees (buyback-and-burn); size burns by a per-booking virality score built on tracked short-link clicks plus impression lift. See 09-token.md.

## Consequences
- Supersedes the "no token of our own" line in 01-scope.md (now "not in MVP").
- Requires: legal opinion, virality tracking (short links) in Phase 2, a public burn log.
